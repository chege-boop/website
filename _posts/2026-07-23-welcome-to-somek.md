Welcome to the official digital home of the Society of Marine Engineers Kenya (SOMEK). 

As we continue our mission to advance the marine engineering profession in Kenya, this platform will serve as our central hub for news, advocacy updates, and member resources.

### What to Expect
- Regular updates on our ongoing international MOU lobbying efforts.
- Important guidelines and mentorship steps regarding EBK registration.
- Announcements for upcoming technical seminars and maritime conventions.

We encourage all licensed engine officers, surveyors, and maritime professionals to check back frequently. Together, we are driving industry excellence and building a sustainable blue economy for Kenya!

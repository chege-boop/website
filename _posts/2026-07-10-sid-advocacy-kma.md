The SOMEK executive team is actively addressing the delays surrounding the issuance of the Seafarers Identity Document (SID).

We have received reports from several members facing disembarkation challenges and unfair penalties at international ports due to the lack of this crucial document. In response, SOMEK has drafted formal communications to the Director General of the Kenya Maritime Authority (KMA) urging for the immediate expediting of the SID process.

We are committed to ensuring our members can work globally without administrative hindrances. We will keep the society updated on KMA's response.

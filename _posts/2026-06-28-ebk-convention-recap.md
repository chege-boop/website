SOMEK was proudly represented at the 7th EBK Engineering Convention. The event provided an excellent platform to network with industry leaders and advocate for the maritime sector.

### Key Takeaways
- **MOU Progress:** We highlighted the successful signing of the Singapore and South Korea MOUs, and pushed for the fast-tracking of remaining agreements to open up global job markets for Kenyan seafarers.
- **Shipbuilding Initiative:** Preliminary discussions were held regarding potential shipbuilding and maintenance training programs, drawing inspiration from industry experts like Eng. Michael Vallez.

Thank you to all members whose contributions made our attendance and advocacy at this convention possible!

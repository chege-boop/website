Our Mission

Setting the Standard for Maritime Excellence.

SOMEK is committed to safeguarding the interests of marine engineers while promoting standard practices in Kenya's blue economy. We collaborate with the KMA, EBK, and international bodies to secure global recognition for our members.

  • Advocacy for International MOUs and fair employment.
  • Mentorship programs for EBK Professional Registration.
  • Continuous Technical Professional Development.

Membership Requirements

Join a network of certified professionals driving Kenya's maritime future.

Corporate Member

Holds a recognized degree or CoC (Class 1 or 2).

Graduate Member

Holds a recognized Marine Engineering degree.

Student Member

Currently enrolled in an approved marine program.
